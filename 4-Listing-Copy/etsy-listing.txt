DESCRIPTION
-----------
Sprout is a cute, minimal Shopify theme made for pet shops — dog food, cat food, treats, beds, toys and accessories. Shoppers pick their pet once, find the right size in seconds, and build bundles in one click.

★ WHAT'S INCLUDED
• Shop by Pet — dog, puppy, cat or small pet; the store stays filtered to them
• Size & portion finder — weight slider (kg or lb) suggests the right pack
• Bundle builder — shoppers tick items and add the whole set at once
• Happy-pets photo wall with "pet of the week"
• Slide-out cart with a free-delivery progress bar
• Wishlist hearts + sticky add-to-bag bar
• Trust badges, subscribe & gifting blocks, newsletter footer
• 29 custom sections — home, product, collection, cart, blog, contact, gifting, 404
• Mobile-first, Online Store 2.0 — edit everything in the theme editor, no code

★ HOW IT WORKS
1. Download the ZIP after purchase
2. Shopify Admin → Online Store → Themes → Import → Upload zip file
3. Add your logo, products and photos, then customise
A step-by-step setup video is included with your download.
